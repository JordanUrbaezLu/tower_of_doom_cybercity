## Why nothing was converted or zoned

The plan was to convert the 77 Greyhound `.seanim` clips and zone them under
their stock names. The first check made that unnecessary and unwise:

- **All 77 clips already ship in `zm_common.ff`.** The Mod Tools'
  `zone_source/all/assetlist/zm_common.csv` lists every one of the 48
  `pb_staff_*` and 29 `pt_staff_*` xanims (`zm_common_staff_clips.log`).
- **Every Zombies usermap loads `zm_common` before its own fastfile.** The
  user's own Tower of Doom console log (2026-09-23) shows
  `core_common` -> `zm_patch` -> `zm_common` -> `zm_levelcommon` ->
  `zm_tower_of_doom` (`fastfile_load_order.log`).
- **The playeranim scripts ship in `core_common.ff`** (all 13 `.script`
  rawfiles + `playeranimtypes.txt`, `core_common_playeranim.log`), and the
  ZM block of `playeranim_melee.script` binds `pt_staff_{prone,crouch,stand}_melee`
  (and `_melee_charge`) under `playerAnimType armminigun` beneath a
  `// STAFF` comment (`playeranim_melee_zm_staff.log`). The loco/fire scripts
  were not dumped, but zm_common's pb_ families are exactly bow 135, sword 73,
  spikes 69, **staff 48**, i.e. the ZM-only player types bow / sword /
  spikelauncher / armminigun, and the melee script names armminigun as STAFF.
- `armminigun` is a native type (`share/raw/gamedata/playeranim/playeranimtypes.txt`)
  and `deffiles/projectileweapon.awi` offers it for a projectile weapon.

So Treyarch's own compiled clips are already resident in every match. A
map-made copy with the same names would either be skipped as a duplicate or
**shadow** the originals with a reconversion; neither helps. No `.seanim` was
converted, no xanim GDT was written, no zone line was added, and nothing was
added to the `.ff`. `verify_staff_3p.py` fails the build if a repo GDT or
zone/zpkg ever names a `pb_staff_*`/`pt_staff_*` asset.

### The converter recipe, proven but parked

For the record (and in case a future map needs a player anim that zm_common
does NOT carry), the conversion semantics were established against Treyarch's
own export rather than guessed. `pb_rifle_endgame_{1st,2nd,3rd}place_idle`
exist both as Greyhound `.seanim` and as Treyarch's `XANIM_BIN` in the tools
(`xanim_export/mp/t7_mp/endgame/`). FK of the seanim on
`model_export/base_character/bo3_mp_character_fullbody.XMODEL_BIN` matches the
shipped export on every frame to <= 0.63 in (typically ~0.01 in on keyed frames;
the rest is Greyhound's sparse keys vs linear resampling) (`endgame_proof.log`):

- positions are centimetres (`/2.54`), the test+map trap;
- translation keys are **offsets from the bone's rest local translation**;
- rotation keys are **absolute local** quaternions (x,y,z,w);
- the game rig has a `torso_stabilizer` between `j_mainroot` and
  `j_spinelower` that the Maya skeleton lacks; its rest frame is tag_origin's.
  Without it the whole upper body is 131 degrees off. Treyarch's stock
  player-anim GDT (`xanim_export/t7_xanim_player_mp.gdt`) uses
  `type mp_fullbody`, `useBones 1`, model `base_character\bo3_mp_character_fullbody.XMODEL_BIN`,
  so the converter synthesises that bone; `pt_` torso clips would be
  `type mp_torso` (the `xanim.awi` combo offers mp_torso / mp_legs / mp_fullbody).
- Header types: every `pb_staff_*` is SEAnim **delta** (30 loop, 18 not),
  every `pt_staff_*` **relative** (only `pt_staff_stand_shellshock` loops),
  all 30 fps. All animated bones exist in the MP base skeleton except
  `torso_stabilizer` (`bone_check.log`).

The probes (`seanim_reader.py`, `fk.py`, `endgame_proof.py`, `mount_tag.py`,
`bone_check.py`) live in `tmp/staff_3p_20260927/probe/`. The only published
third-party precedent found, `_koentje/disk_gun`'s `pt_disc_gun_*`, is a
viewmodel-rig export typed `relative` with `useBones 0` on a
`playerAnimType default` weapon, so the engine never reads it: not a template.

## The mount tag

The docs/160 bow pose mounts on `tag_weapon_left`, because a bow is held in the
left hand. The staff clips carry the staff on the **right** tag
(`mount_tag.log`, 9 clips x 3 frames, tag_origin frame, +X forward):

- `tag_weapon_right` is keyed on every clip, sits ~6.2 in from the right wrist,
  and its **+Z axis points forward and ~15 degrees up**; the LEFT hand lies
  **2.07 in off that +Z line, 22.9 in down it** on every idle / walk / run /
  sprint / crouch / prone / fire frame sampled: a two-handed staff grip along
  the shaft.
- `tag_weapon_left` is a single constant key collapsing it onto the left
  wrist (0.00 in), and a +Z line from it misses the right hand by ~12 in.
- Our world shafts keep their +Z long axis (the Origins shaft is Treyarch's
  own staff world model, -24.29..47.65 in Z; the ice body was built in the same
  frame, docs/160). Right hand at Z 0, left hand at Z ~23, tip ~47 ahead.

Pose and mount are a PAIR: `verify_staff_presentation.py` and
`verify_staff_3p.py` accept armminigun + right or bow + left, never a mix.

## Unproven - what only the game can answer

1. **That armminigun drives the LOCOMOTION set on a usermap player.** The melee
   binding is read from the script; the loco/fire/raise bindings are inferred
   (zm_common's pb_ families + the `// STAFF` comment). If the body still
   holds a generic pose while walking, that inference is wrong.
2. **The `#demo_version_at_least 28` guard** around the ZM staff melee block
   is assumed satisfied by the current retail build.
3. **The look.** The clips were authored for Treyarch's staff world model on
   the player rig; this map's bodies are stock ZM bodies on the same rig, and
   the fire/lightning shafts are the Origins model, but nobody has seen it.
4. The dev log label in `_tod_classes.gsc` still prints `cfg_pose=bow
   cfg_hand=left` (it is a hardcoded config label, not a readback). Scripts
   were out of scope for this change; update that string to
   `cfg_pose=armminigun cfg_hand=right` with the next script edit.

**Fallback (one edit):** in `tools/staff_presentation_bindings.py` set
`STAFF_PLAYER_ANIM_TYPE = 'bow'` and `STAFF_WORLD_MOUNT = 'tag_weapon_left'`,
refresh, regenerate, FULL build (a GDT edit). Both validators accept that pair.

## What to look at in game

Use the dev Mage dummy (`tod_dev` spawns it and cycles all six staff forms,
docs/160) or a co-op partner, and watch from the side and front:

- **Idle** standing, crouched and prone: staff held across the body in both
  hands, pointing forward and slightly up; left hand ON the shaft.
- **Walk / run / sprint**, incl. strafing and backwards (partner only - the
  dummy stands still): legs animate, staff stays in both hands.
- **Crouch / prone transitions and crawl.**
- **Fire** (each staff), **raise** after a weapon swap, **melee** and the
  charged melee, **flinch** when hit.
- **Staff head placement** on all six forms: base and packed heads on the tip
  end, not at the hand (docs/160 sockets).

Anything that looks like a rifle hold or a bow hold means the armminigun
lookup is not reaching these clips (unproven item 1): report it with a
screenshot and fall back as above.
